title: Bike Trip to Goa
description: 1,400 km on two wheels from Bangalore to Goa and back, with far too many chai stops.
date: 2017-10-07
tags: [travel, life]
---
Four of us, four bikes, one long weekend. The plan was simple: leave Bangalore at 4am, reach Goa by sunset.

The plan did not survive contact with the Chitradurga highway.

> "We'll stop for just one chai." — everyone, every forty minutes.

Still one of the best trips I've done.
